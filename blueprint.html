Turn a VIN and glass type into structured vehicle and part information.

VIN→Playwright→MyGrant→Parts
  • Typed VIN intake
  • Windshield or Back Glass
  • Vehicle year, make, and model
  • Primary, interchangeable, and OEM part numbers

NEXT DISCOVERY

What we need to learn next

These questions prevent us from encoding assumptions as business rules.

01

How is a quote calculated?

Glass cost, labor, molding, tax, calibration, travel, markup, and channel-specific pricing.

02

What happens after approval?

Address capture, inventory check, ordering, appointment selection, and customer confirmation.

03

How does auction work differ?

Batch size, authorization, vehicle location, service scheduling, and invoice structure.

04

How is work completed and proven?

Technician assignment, before/after photos, signatures, exceptions, and quality checks.

05

How does each channel pay?

Payment method, terms, reconciliation, insurance documentation, and overdue follow-up.
